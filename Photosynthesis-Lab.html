MeasureValue

A teaching model of one square metre of a healthy leaf, built from the standard biochemical model of photosynthesis (rubisco limited and electron transport limited rates, with photorespiration and respiration) and rounded, typical values. Light is photosynthetically active photon flux. Monochromatic light uses the action spectrum of a thin layer of cells; thick leaves use green light somewhat better than this.
