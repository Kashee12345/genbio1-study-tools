    MeasureValue

    pH is solved exactly from a charge balance, using a carbonic acid pKa of 6.1 (the value used for blood, which counts dissolved CO₂), 10.3 for bicarbonate and water's ion product of 1.0 × 10⁻¹⁴, so pure water starts at pH 7.00. The buffered beaker starts like blood plasma: 24 mM bicarbonate and 1.2 mM carbonic acid. "Lungs on" holds carbonic acid at 1.2 mM by breathing extra CO₂ away (an open system). Each portion adds 1 mM of strong acid (HCl) or base (NaOH). Proteins and phosphate buffers are left out. The particle view uses a log scale for H⁺.
