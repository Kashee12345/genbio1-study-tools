MeasureValue

Simplified model for learning, with time sped up. cAMP = 1 minus the glucose level. CAP occupancy rises with cAMP. The share of time the operator is free rises with lactose (allolactose) for a normal repressor. Transcription = operator free × (5% without CAP, up to 100% with CAP). Beta galactosidase activity heads toward 1000 × transcription units. Left out: inducer exclusion (glucose also limits lactose entry), the permease feedback loop, the need for some beta galactosidase to make allolactose, and the three extra operators of the real lac region.

    Part 2: Eukaryotic Dial Board

    Methyl group on DNAAcetyl group on histonesNucleosome Activators and RISCmRNA and miRNAProtein
    Eukaryotic gene readouts
    MeasureValue

    Simplified model for learning. Chromatin access = (0.15 + 0.85 × acetylation) × (1 − 0.9 × methylation). Activator effect = activators ÷ 4 × (1 − 0.7 × methylation), since methyl groups block binding. Transcription = access × (0.05 + 0.95 × activator effect). mRNA level = transcription × (1 − 0.5 × miRNA). Translation = 1 − 0.85 × miRNA. Protein = mRNA × translation. Real genes differ in how strongly each dial matters.
