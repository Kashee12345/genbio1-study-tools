A teaching model, not a real cell line. Phase lengths are typical for cultured human cells (G1 about 10 h, S 8 h, G2 4 h, M about 1 h) with random variation, and time is sped up (simulated hours per second shown). Cells are drawn with 2n = 4. The dish holds about 140 cells in one layer. Growth factor below about 15% sends cells to G0. Damaged cells with normal p53 pause in G1 or G2 to repair, and some undergo apoptosis; with mutant p53 the G1 stop and apoptosis fail and only a brief G2 pause remains, so damage is passed on. Spindle poisons hold cells at the M checkpoint, and long arrests end in cell death. Overexpressed G1 cyclin pushes cells past the G1 checkpoint even without growth factor or space (simplified).
