The rate follows the Michaelis-Menten equation, v = Vmax[S] ÷ (Km + [S]), with Km = 2 mM and Vmax = 10 µmol/min for 1 unit of enzyme at its best temperature and pH. A competitive inhibitor multiplies Km by (1 + [I]/Ki); a pure noncompetitive inhibitor divides Vmax by (1 + [N]/Ki). Temperature uses Q10 = 2 below the optimum and denaturation above it; pH uses a bell curve around the optimum. Denaturation here is shown as instant and reversible so you can explore; real heat denaturation is often permanent. Animation speed is exaggerated.
