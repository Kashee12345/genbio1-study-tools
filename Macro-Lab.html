    A two-state teaching model: stability is a score in kcal/mol built from the hydrophobic core, salt bridges, a disulfide bridge, exposed nonpolar R groups, temperature and pH; fraction folded = 1 / (1 + e−stability/RT). Real proteins have many more interactions, and acidic and basic R groups gain or lose charge gradually around their pKa values. The 16-residue chain stands in for a real enzyme.
